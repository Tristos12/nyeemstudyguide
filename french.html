🇫🇷 Form 1 French

Term 1 Examination Preparation

Based on the uploaded Babonneau Secondary School November 2026 Form 1 French examination. Learn greetings, classroom language, days and dates, personal conversations, family questions and friendly letters.

Days 1–3: 60 practice questions daily. Day 4: 75-question untimed and 60-question timed mastery tests, and a 36-mark school-style paper with three sections and a suggested one-hour duration.

Writing is marked by an adult using the supplied 20-mark guide. Progress saves separately under each learner profile in this browser.
