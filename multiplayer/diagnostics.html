Multiplayer Connection Check

Teacher and administrator diagnostic screen

Check the online multiplayer service

This checks whether the separate Vercel service can connect to the dedicated Neon database and find the required room tables. It does not create rooms, change student data, or require a password.

The connection request is made from your browser to the multiplayer API. No learner information is sent.

Not tested in this browser.

What the results mean

ReadyThe server and multiplayer tables respond
Not configuredA required environment setting is missing
Schema missingDatabase is connected but room tables are missing
Connection blockedNetwork, deployment protection, or browser access may be preventing the request

A successful connection check does not mean a full two-learner match has passed testing. The student-facing multiplayer game remains separate until that test is completed.
