🔢 Form 1 Grade 7 Mathematics

Sets · Number Systems · Algebra · Geometry · Introductory Trigonometry

Four-day examination preparation

Days 1–3: 12 questions in each of five sections, for 60 per day. Day 4: a 75-question untimed test and a 60-question test timed for 1 hour 15 minutes. Mastery requires at least 90% on each test.

The first four sections include essential Grade 7 skills. Basic right-triangle trigonometry is offered as an introductory extension, not as a claim about the official school examination.
