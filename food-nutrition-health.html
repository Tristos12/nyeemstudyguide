🥗 Form 1 Food, Nutrition and Health

Term 1 Examination Preparation

This revision covers all four sections of the uploaded Babonneau Secondary School examination. Days 1–3 each have 60 questions across five strands. Day 4 includes a 75-question untimed mastery test, a 60-question timed mastery test (75 minutes), and a separate school-style paper using the original section types.

The paper labels Sections A–D and gives a one-hour duration. Its displayed Section A, C and D mark subtotals are 20, 12 and 31; Section B asks for five kitchen-rule violations without stating a mark total. Our practice allocates one mark to each identified violation, creating a 68-mark practice paper. These are new questions assessing the same skills.
