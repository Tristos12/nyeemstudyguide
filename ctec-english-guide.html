Jan 2027: Main ideas and information

Your learning focus for this month

Week 1 · Purpose and audience

Read one purpose and audience example; annotate key language choices; complete one short comprehension set; draft and revise a 120–180-word response.

Practise this week: 3 real activities

Read the passage

At Babonneau Secondary School, 34 students joined a lunchtime reading club. The group met twice weekly. Teachers reported greater participation, but no formal reading assessment had yet been completed.

  1. Identify the intended audience and purpose, giving one reason for each.
  2. Select two details that must be included in a formal announcement.
  3. Write a 150-word notice, report, letter or email based on the passage.

Your written response

View success criteria

Suitable format, clear purpose, accurate facts, logical order and appropriate tone.
