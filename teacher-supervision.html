Teacher Supervision & Safeguarding

Babonneau Secondary School · Form 1 Learning Hub · Classroom pilot

Scope of teacher supervision

This is a teacher guidance page, not a staff login or live moderation console. It does not open student inboxes, read private chats, identify learners or retrieve room records. The current four-digit learner code is not secure authentication and must never be used as proof of teacher identity.

Use a supervised session with school-approved procedures and responsible adult oversight. The platform does not automatically notify staff when a learner reports a message. Teachers must use existing school reporting channels for concerns.

Before a class challenge

0 of 6 preparation steps checked.

During the match

Keep the activity visible to the supervising teacher. Use predetermined partners or small supervised groups. Encourage ready-made Quick Chat messages over typed messages. If a learner reports discomfort, instruct them to hide typed chat or leave the match and speak directly with a staff member.

Do not ask learners to disclose private room tokens, inbox secret keys or four-digit learner codes. Do not copy personal conversations into a shared class group.

Responding to a concern

  1. Prioritise the learner's immediate safety; stop the activity or separate participants when necessary.
  2. Use the game's Report & hide control if there is an inappropriate typed message. The room hides the reported content, but this does not constitute a report to school staff.
  3. Notify the designated school safeguarding lead or school administration through the school's established process. If there is an urgent threat, use the applicable emergency response procedure.
  4. Record only the necessary facts in the school's approved, access-controlled incident system. Do not enter learner incidents into this public webpage.
  5. Follow school procedures for parent contact, support, evidence handling and further action.

Current technical boundaries

The live game currently uses private invitations and room-specific access. There is no authenticated staff moderation role, central teacher inbox, independent safeguarding alert, or school-wide report archive in this interface. Room invitations may be forwarded, so private links are not a substitute for student identity verification.

Before broader deployment, the school should approve a secure staff sign-in, server-enforced moderation permissions, documented retention and deletion rules, clear reporting escalation, and a privacy assessment appropriate to minors.
