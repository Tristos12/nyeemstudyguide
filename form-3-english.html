Paper 2: Reading and Writing Skills

The uploaded paper assesses comprehension through a factual passage about the octopus, guided formal-letter writing requesting an educational visit to Pigeon Island, and a school club notice. The activities here practise the same skills with new prompts.

A. Reading comprehension · 16-mark skills

Read the new practice passage, then answer the nine questions in complete sentences where appropriate.

Protecting the Hawksbill Turtle At dawn, volunteers walked along a quiet Caribbean beach to check for sea turtle nests. The hawksbill turtle has a narrow, pointed beak that helps it reach food in crevices around coral reefs. Although it spends most of its life at sea, the female returns to land to lay her eggs in the sand. Hawksbill turtles face several dangers. Plastic waste may be mistaken for food, bright lights can confuse newly hatched turtles, and damaged coral reefs reduce access to feeding areas. Conservation groups respond by organising beach clean-ups, protecting nesting areas and teaching visitors how to behave responsibly near wildlife. A young volunteer named Leila noticed fresh tracks leading from the sea towards the vegetation. Rather than disturb the nest, she recorded the location and informed her supervisor. The team marked a safe boundary and reminded beach visitors to keep their distance. Their careful actions helped protect the nesting area without interrupting the turtle.

B. Formal letter writing · 20 marks

Practice task: As Form 3 class representative, write to your principal requesting permission for an educational visit to a Saint Lucian heritage site. Explain what students will learn, how the visit supports English and Social Studies, and how the class will behave responsibly.

Letter structure

  1. Sender's address and date
  2. Recipient's designation and address
  3. Formal salutation
  4. Subject line
  5. Introduction and purpose
  6. Reasons and expected learning
  7. Respectful request and closing
  8. Signature and role

Subject: Request for Permission to Undertake an Educational Visit

Dear Principal,

I am writing on behalf of my Form 3 class to request permission to visit a Saint Lucian heritage site as part of our learning programme. We believe the visit would help us develop a better understanding of our country's history while strengthening our observation and report-writing skills.

Use this as guidance, not a complete letter to copy. Add your own clear reasons, proposed arrangements and closing.

Paper 2: Written Mock Examination

Complete all three sections. The total is 51 marks. Each section has its own draft box. Save your responses before leaving this page.

Section A · Reading Comprehension (16 marks)

A Community Library Project A group of Form 3 volunteers noticed that books donated to a small community library were difficult to find. Some books had been placed in boxes, while others had no labels. The volunteers decided to sort the collection into fiction, information texts and reference materials. Before moving any books, they asked the librarian for guidance. The librarian explained that visitors needed clear signs and an accurate list of titles. Over two Saturdays, students labelled shelves, recorded book titles and prepared signs. They also set aside damaged books for the librarian to examine. When the library reopened, visitors could locate books more easily. The volunteers realised that careful planning, teamwork and respect for shared resources can make a difference in the community.

Answer in complete sentences where appropriate.

Section B · Formal Letter (20 marks)

Write a formal letter to your principal requesting permission for your Form 3 class to organise an educational reading event for younger students. Explain the purpose, proposed activities, benefits and arrangements. Include all parts of a formal letter.

Section C · Notice Writing (15 marks)

Your class is starting an after-school Reading and Debate Club. Prepare an attractive written notice to recruit members, giving the club's purpose, activities, eligibility, meeting date, time, place, what to bring, registration details and a contact person.
