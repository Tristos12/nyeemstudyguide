📐 Form 1 Technical Drawing

Term 1 revision and practical constructions

Based on the uploaded Babonneau Secondary School Form 1 Technical Drawing examination. Days 1–3 each have 60 questions across five topics. Day 4 has a 75-question untimed test, 60-question timed mastery test (75 minutes), and a school-style 50-mark, one-hour practice paper.

Compass constructions must be completed with a real pencil, compass and ruler on paper, then marked by an adult or teacher. Online diagrams illustrate the parts, not the original examination diagram.
