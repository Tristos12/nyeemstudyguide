Privacy and safety

No student directory, email address or phone number is needed. Sender nicknames are not identity-verified. Only accept invitations from someone you recognise. If an invitation feels uncomfortable, dismiss it and tell a trusted adult.

An inbox is linked only to the learner profile on this browser, not an official school account. Messages appear here and in the Learning Hub while those pages are open. These are in-page notifications, not phone push alerts. Deleting the browser's saved data may remove access to an existing inbox. Never share your private inbox key.
