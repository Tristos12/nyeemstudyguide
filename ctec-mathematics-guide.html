Guided sample task for Module 1

A jacket costs EC$120 and is discounted by 15%. Find the saving and sale price.

See suggested answer or method

15% of 120 = 0.15 × 120 = 18. Sale price = EC$102. Check: EC$18 + EC$102 = EC$120.

This is a school-created practice example, not an official CXC examination question.

Guided sample task for Module 2

Scores are 6, 9, 7, 8 and 10. Calculate the mean and state what it represents.

See suggested answer or method

Mean = (6+9+7+8+10) ÷ 5 = 8. It describes the average score for this set.

This is a school-created practice example, not an official CXC examination question.

My progress and certification checklist

Track your monthly completion.

School and CXC requirements

  1. Confirm that your teacher has checked the full current syllabus objectives, not only these practice summaries.
  2. Ask the school's examination coordinator to confirm registration availability and permitted modular entries each year.
  3. Check the SBA or alternative assessment requirements for the first official entry, deadlines, and any permitted transfers.
  4. Complete school mock assessments and retain evidence of completed learning tasks.
  5. Sit official CTEC module examinations as approved, and record the result for each module.
  6. After the final sitting, have the school verify all three module results and certification status with CXC.

Learning checkmarks are private, device-specific planning aids. They do not establish official eligibility, CTEC credit or certification. No student names or accounts are collected here.
