Your pathway at a glance

CTEC recognises achievement in separately assessed subject modules. This school plan groups learning into three years so students can see exactly what to learn, practise and review. Formal CTEC certification requires CXC assessments, not simply completing these website activities.

Form 3 · Module 1

Fundamentals of Secondary Level Mathematics

Build dependable number sense, financial numeracy, measurement and introductory algebra.

Term 1 · Number theory and computation

What to learn
  • Whole numbers, integers, fractions, decimals and percentages
  • Factors, multiples, prime numbers, index notation and order of operations
  • Ratio, proportion, estimation and checking reasonableness
  • Use number skills in Caribbean everyday situations
Student work: Complete a weekly mixed-arithmetic set, one error log and a ratio/percentage investigation.
End-of-term checkpoint: Achieve at least 80% on a school numeracy check after corrections.

Term 2 · Practical and symbolic mathematics

What to learn
  • Consumer arithmetic: prices, discounts, bills, profit, loss and simple interest
  • Units, conversions, perimeter, area, volume and practical measurement
  • Basic set notation, membership, union, intersection and Venn diagrams
  • Algebra 1: expressions, substitution, simple equations and formulas
Student work: Prepare a mock shopping budget and measurements project; solve five algebra problems twice weekly.
End-of-term checkpoint: Demonstrate accurate working, units and interpretation of results.

Term 3 · Graphs and readiness

What to learn
  • Read and plot coordinate pairs
  • Construct and interpret introductory graphs
  • Solve multistep word problems linking number, algebra and measurement
  • Practise module-style tasks without immediate hints
Student work: Compile six worked-example sheets, two mixed school tests and an error-correction portfolio.
End-of-term checkpoint: Teacher checks the complete Module 1 topic list and two consistent school practice scores.
Form 3 end-of-year readiness review

Student submits corrected practice work, timed samples and an error log. The teacher checks understanding, writing or computation accuracy, independent work and performance across more than one assessment. A school benchmark is a preparation target, not an official CXC passing grade.

Form 4 · Module 2

Intermediate Secondary Level Mathematics

Apply statistics, algebra, graphs, geometry, trigonometry and introductory matrices.

Term 1 · Statistics and algebra

What to learn
  • Collect, organise and display data
  • Calculate and interpret mean, median, mode and spread where prescribed
  • Manipulate expressions, equations and algebraic relationships
  • Explain the steps in solutions and identify common errors
Student work: Analyse class survey data and submit a 20-question algebra practice log.
End-of-term checkpoint: Select appropriate statistical methods and solve intermediate algebra questions.

Term 2 · Functions, geometry and trigonometry

What to learn
  • Relations, functions and coordinate graphs
  • Gradient, linear relationships and graphical interpretation
  • Geometric properties, angles and constructions
  • Right-triangle trigonometry and context-based geometry problems
Student work: Produce a graphing investigation and a geometry/trigonometry problem set with diagrams.
End-of-term checkpoint: Connect diagrams, formulas and reasoning in a multistep solution.

Term 3 · Vectors, matrices and review

What to learn
  • Introduce vector representation, magnitude and operations
  • Use basic matrices and their applications as defined by the syllabus
  • Solve mixed problems from Statistics 1, Algebra 2 and Graphs 1
  • Complete teacher-marked module assessments
Student work: Create a structured revision folder and complete two school-made mixed mocks.
End-of-term checkpoint: Teacher reviews Module 1 status and Module 2 proficiency before official entry.
Form 4 end-of-year readiness review

Student submits corrected practice work, timed samples and an error log. The teacher checks understanding, writing or computation accuracy, independent work and performance across more than one assessment. A school benchmark is a preparation target, not an official CXC passing grade.

Form 5 · Module 3

Higher Concepts in Secondary Level Mathematics

Integrate higher-order mathematical ideas and justify accurate multistep solutions.

Term 1 · Advanced data and functions

What to learn
  • Study Statistics 2 and interpretation of more complex data
  • Relations, functions and graphs 2
  • Interpret nonlinear or advanced graphical representations where prescribed
  • Model real-life situations and assess the suitability of answers
Student work: Complete one data investigation, six function tasks and a personal misconception log.
End-of-term checkpoint: Explain how graphs and data support mathematical conclusions.

Term 2 · Higher geometry, vectors and matrices

What to learn
  • Geometry and Trigonometry 2 including more demanding triangle problems
  • Transformations and geometric reasoning
  • Vectors and Matrices 2 and applications
  • Choose strategies for unfamiliar, multi-step problems
Student work: Complete a geometry modelling task and two extended mixed-problem sets.
End-of-term checkpoint: Show correct notation, method and explanation with minimal prompting.

Term 3 · Examination readiness

What to learn
  • Complete timed module-style objective and structured practice
  • Review weak skills from all three modules
  • Use error analysis to refine methods and time management
  • Confirm assessment, registration and certification record with the school
Student work: Complete two school mock cycles, a revision checklist and individual teacher conference.
End-of-term checkpoint: Review all module results; identify outstanding requirements or permitted resit options.
Form 5 end-of-year readiness review

Student submits corrected practice work, timed samples and an error log. The teacher checks understanding, writing or computation accuracy, independent work and performance across more than one assessment. A school benchmark is a preparation target, not an official CXC passing grade.

Weekly learning routine

Learn

Two 30–40 minute sessions reviewing a concept, model or worked example.

Practise

Two independent sessions completing mixed problems or comprehension and writing tasks.

Check and improve

One session correcting errors, retesting skills and recording one next target.

Recommended supplemental study time only. Teachers should adjust workload to students' support needs and the school timetable.

From school readiness to CTEC certification

  1. Review the latest CXC syllabus for all stated learning outcomes.
  2. Ask the school's examinations coordinator which modular sittings and entry routes are available for your cohort.
  3. Confirm permitted examination entry for your age/form and the proposed module sequence.
  4. Complete the required SBA or permitted alternative paper according to current CXC rules, including first-sitting obligations.
  5. Sit the required official modular papers and retain formal results.
  6. Check what each module result means and whether a resit is needed or allowed.
  7. After the last module, ask the examinations coordinator to verify the complete credential record with CXC.

Assessment policy alert: CXC lists SBA or alternative SBA arrangements for the 2027 CTEC English A and Mathematics offerings. CXC has also announced changes to SBA policy, so the school should recheck the entry rules every year.
