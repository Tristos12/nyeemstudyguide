How CTEC Mathematics works

CTEC recognises successful learning in individual modules. The CXC Mathematics syllabus is structured into three modules, moving from foundational number and measurement ideas towards intermediate applications and higher concepts.

This is a school-developed learning guide, not an official CXC syllabus or examination paper. The topics below are study summaries; the official syllabus defines the full learning outcomes and assessment requirements.

Module 1: Fundamentals of Secondary Level Mathematics

Learning goal: Calculate accurately, choose appropriate units and solve straightforward numerical problems in everyday settings.

Worked example: percentages

A school bag costs EC$80. A shop offers a 15% discount.

15% of 80 = 0.15 × 80 = 12. Discounted price = 80 − 12 = EC$68.

Check: the reduced price must be lower than the original price.

Try three questions
  1. Find 25% of 240.
  2. Convert 3.5 metres to centimetres.
  3. Solve 4x + 3 = 23.
Answers and explanations
  1. 60, because 0.25 × 240 = 60.
  2. 350 cm, because 1 m = 100 cm.
  3. x = 5, because 4x = 20.

Module 2: Intermediate Secondary Level Mathematics

Learning goal: Interpret information, work confidently with algebra and use geometry to solve problems.

Worked example: mean

A student records quiz scores of 6, 8, 7, 9 and 10. The mean is the total divided by the number of scores.

(6 + 8 + 7 + 9 + 10) ÷ 5 = 8.

Try two questions
  1. Calculate the mean of 4, 10, 7 and 11.
  2. Find the area of a triangle with base 12 cm and perpendicular height 7 cm.
Answers and explanations
  1. 8, because the total 32 is divided by 4.
  2. 42 cm², because area = ½ × 12 × 7.

Module 3: Higher Concepts in Secondary Level Mathematics

Learning goal: Work with more advanced mathematical representations and justify multi-step solutions.

Worked example: simple probability

A bag contains three red counters and two blue counters. There are five equally likely counters in total. The probability of drawing a red counter is 3/5, or 0.6.

Try two questions
  1. If f(x) = 2x + 5, find f(4).
  2. List the intersection of A = {1, 2, 3, 4} and B = {3, 4, 5}.
Answers and explanations
  1. 13, because 2(4) + 5 = 13.
  2. {3, 4}, the values shared by both sets.

Suggested mathematics study routine

Day 1: Read a worked example and explain it aloud. Day 2: Solve five similar problems. Day 3: Attempt word problems. Day 4: Practise without notes. Day 5: Check answers, correct errors and seek feedback.
