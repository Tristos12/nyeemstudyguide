Paper 2: Three full examinations

Each paper includes nine comprehension questions (16 marks), a complete formal letter (20 marks), and a notice (15 marks). All sections must be completed for a full Paper 2 submission.

Mock examinations

Four separate mock examinations are provided. Each type has an untimed option and a timed option.

Assessment and safeguarding

Timed papers run against the device clock; an expired paper is automatically submitted with any unanswered questions left blank. Writing is never automatically awarded marks. These are revision activities, not official examination grades or supervised assessments.
