Prepare for the Term 1 Physical Education Examination

Based on the uploaded Babonneau Secondary School First Term PE paper. The examination allows 45 minutes and covers 50 marks across Section A (10 multiple-choice marks), Section B (25 structured marks) and Section C (15 marks for a 75–100-word Sports Club election speech).

These are original revision papers, not official examinations. Written responses require teacher assessment; locally saved progress stays on this browser.

The source exam asks students to select two sports for the organisation table, but shows three rows and awards 9 marks. These revision papers explicitly request three sports. The source also uses fixed ages for puberty; these lessons explain that development varies among individuals.
